# Eating at the cashier workstation

Basis: user-provided workplace policy, supplied 2026-10-18.

The user explicitly requires that cashiers must not eat at the workplace/table during work and that visible eating there be identified as a violation. This record documents that instruction. It does not claim an independently verified employer policy, public-law provision, or statutory citation.

The application applies rule `cashier_eating_at_workstation` when referenced video frames establish identifiable food consumption by the person operating the cashier workstation, with visible working context. Evidence must show a food-to-mouth and bite/chew sequence and its relationship to the operating desk. A single hand-to-face gesture is insufficient.

Drinking alone, food merely present or handled, touching the face, medicine taking, ambiguous small objects, eating outside the workstation, and a break-room or unresolved nonworking context do not establish this violation. The application does not infer a person's shift schedule or identity from appearance. Unreadable or incomplete context remains unknown.

Supported events include the source timestamp and evidence JPEGs. They remain subject to human review. Model confidence is uncalibrated; the policy does not claim measured detection accuracy.
