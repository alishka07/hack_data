Included runtime sources and weights

RT-DETR / RT-DETRv2
  Upstream: https://github.com/lyuwenyu/RT-DETR
  Code: runtime/RT-DETR-main/rtdetrv2_pytorch/src and configs
  License: Apache-2.0, full text in runtime/RT-DETR-main/LICENSE
  COCO checkpoint: rtdetrv2_r50vd_6x_coco_ema.pth
  Local adapter: run_local.py; offline checkpoint loading and device selection.
  models/equipment/best.pth is a local four-class fine-tune of this architecture.
  It is experimental and does not establish the accuracy of banking decisions.

ByteTrack
  Upstream: https://github.com/ifzhang/ByteTrack
  License: MIT, full text in runtime/ByteTrack-main/LICENSE
  Included: tracking subset, not YOLOX detector/training packages.
  Local changes: minimal yolox/__init__.py; NumPy float64 compatibility;
  matching.py uses local bbox.py in place of the Cython extension, preserving
  inclusive-pixel IoU; run_rtdetr_bytetrack.py is the local RT-DETR adapter
  with full-video processing, timeline metadata and browser-compatible export.

Grounding DINO tiny
  Model: https://huggingface.co/IDEA-Research/grounding-dino-tiny
  License: Apache-2.0; model card and tokenizer files accompany the weights.
  Full Apache license: runtime/GroundingDINO-local/LICENSE
  Local adapter: detect_local.py, Transformers offline inference.
  Transformers and Tokenizers are installed from their packages, not bundled.

Videos, extracted frames, scene-specific reference images and annotated
training sets are not included in this source/weights distribution.
Dependencies installed by pip retain their respective licenses.
