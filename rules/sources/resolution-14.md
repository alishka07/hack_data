# Resolution 14: reviewed source notes

Official source: [Adilet, V2000020075](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075). Resolution date: 24 February 2020. The existing local legal review dated 17 October 2026 recorded the official Information tab as current, last change 24 December 2024. This note preserves that review; implementation did not independently establish a newer legal status.

Paragraphs 8 and 16 concern isolation of the cash unit and closable/lockable room entrances. Those construction and capability requirements do not by themselves prove that an observed open door breaches a universal always-closed or always-locked requirement. The room category and configuration also need to be established. Consequently, `open_door` is explicitly disabled.

Paragraph 16(4) concerns designated transfer equipment in an applicable cashier-room arrangement. It is not enabled because an open tabletop does not establish that arrangement or prove that the required mechanism has been bypassed.

Several provisions have temporary wording through 31 December 2026 and replacement wording from 1 January 2027. This implementation uses the October 2026 review and does not silently adopt future provisions. Re-review the operative source before use at a later date.
